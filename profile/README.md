# Stratolink

[stratolink.org](https://stratolink.org)

We're building an open source network of small, solar-powered balloons to collect and share data from the stratosphere. Our custom payload weighs just under 10 g. Anyone can build one, launch it, and share their flight on the dashboard.

The [docs](https://stratolink.org/docs/getting-started) cover the build, balloon preparation, radio setup, and launch. Follow the [balloons](https://stratolink.org/dashboard), read our [flight notes and dev logs](https://stratolink.org/blog), or get involved in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Stratolink-hq/discussions).

For research collaborations or bulk data access: [contact@stratolink.org](mailto:contact@stratolink.org).
